What we won't build

  • No feed. Nothing to scroll.
  • No streaks, badges, or "don't break your chain" mechanics.
  • No notifications except the one reminder for the plan you booked.
  • No swiping on people — you're matched on the plan, not ranked by a photo.
  • No countdown timers or fake "only 2 spots left" pressure.
  • Our own success metric is hours spent together in real life, not minutes spent in this app.
